# Kitty Game Ideas backlog (newest first; mark used ideas with the KGI id)

## Used
- KGI-001 Rubber Band Cup Tower: 4 strings on a rubber band, build a cup pyramid without hands.
- KGI-002 Newspaper Dance: pairs dance on a newspaper that gets folded every round.

## Backlog
- Chopstick race: move 10 rajma / chana from one bowl to another with ice-cream sticks as chopsticks in 1 minute.
- Blind bindi: blindfolded, stick a bindi on a face drawn on chart paper; closest to the centre wins.
- Saas says: Simon-says, but every order starts with "Saasu maa kehti hain"; anyone who moves without it is out.
- Dupatta knot relay: teams tie and untie dupatta knots down the line, fastest team wins.
- Balloon between foreheads: pairs carry a balloon between their foreheads to the finish line, no hands.
- Spoon lemon saree walk: classic lemon race, but everyone in a saree and walking backwards.
- Mehendi pictionary: draw the word with a mehendi cone on paper, team guesses in 30 seconds.
- Housie with a twist: every called number has a dare, the one who cuts it does the dare.
- Ek minute makeup: blindfolded partner does your lipstick in one minute, group votes the best look.
- Pass the parcel rhymes: when the music stops, finish the Bollywood line the host starts.
