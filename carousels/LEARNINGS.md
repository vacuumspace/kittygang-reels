# Kitty Game Ideas: what works (learning loop)

Read this every run before picking a game. Update it every Monday (IST) from `autopilot/tracker.json` -> `carousels`
(stats: views, reach, saves, shares, comments, skip %, watched %) and `autopilot/data/trends.json`. Newest first, under 60 lines.

## 2026-11-26 (seed, from 34 carousels KGI-001..034 and hashtag research)
Our carousels were weak: 150-750 views, almost no comments. Moving to reels where the gang PLAYS the game.
- Best scores: Katori Housie (KGI-026, score 10.1, 5 saves, 2 shares), Pencil Bottle Drop (7.1), Kuch Nahi Chahiye (7.0),
  Teen Shaadi Ek Shaam (6.8), Rubber Band Cup Tower (KGI-001, 750 views, 5 saves), Taash Housie (5.9), Kitty Awards (5.6).
  Pattern: Housie with a twist, and quick physical challenges with a visible result.
- Worst (score 0): wedding-season craft/word games (Mandap Banao, Shaadi Akshar, Joota Chupai, Sangeet Ki Practice),
  photo guessing. Too much setup, no funny moment to watch.
- Outside our page, the biggest kitty game posts are Housie variants ("Housie quiz" 67K likes, "Normal tambola sab
  khelte hain, aaj kuch hatke" 156 comments) and one-minute challenges with a silly payoff (blindfold lipstick,
  stack the cups, pass the pearl on a spoon). Real ladies laughing at a fail is the hook.
- Do more: a Housie twist game every 3rd game; one-minute challenges; a twist that makes someone fail in a funny way;
  props every home has; an argument topic everyone has at home (vrat rules, saas rules, who pays).
- Do less: games that need drawing, writing or long setup; wedding crafts; games you can't SEE being funny.
- Reel shape to test first: play first (hook scene mid-game), 2-3 fast boards, play scene with a fail, winner + confetti.
  Watch skip % (hook) and watched % (middle). If watched % falls during boards, cut boards to 2.
