# Theme Housie reel learnings

Nightly reel of the day's booked theme ("POV: game night"), built from the theme pack: its art, music and number calls.
The researcher routine adds a dated line here when the stats show something, and tunes `theme/config.json`.

## Team rules
- Cover: theme name, date, all public game times of the day. No "Public Housie" on the cover.
- Ending: times card, then the Download "Kitty Party" app card (Play Store / App Store) in the day's theme. Always.
- Only real app screens and the theme's own art; never invent app features.
