# Learnings (newest first). Only real learnings from team feedback or post data.

## 2026-10-26 · Monday review (stats as of 2026-10-25)
- Types so far: every reel with data is a sitcom; group-chat (KG-2026-10-26-a) and door-arrivals (KG-2026-10-26-b) are queued, compare them next Monday.
- Winner by far: Diwali safai wali Housie (KG-2026-10-22-c): 1161 views, 5 shares, 5 saves, skip 55.5%, watched 26%. A Housie game with a festival twist the audience lives right now.
- Weak: Bollywood theme (278 views, skip 78%), Diwali gift exchange (284, skip 59%), Sirf ek piece (97, skip 71%). No shares or saves on any of them.
- Hook: the one reel under 56% skip opened on a game in progress (cage, ball, "Mera!"). Openings with a guest at the door or a costume reveal lost 6-7 in 10 in 3 s. Start on the game or the conflict, not an entrance pose.
- Middle: watched % is 9-26%; even the winner loses most viewers by 9 s. Keep a new beat every 3 s and a visible stake chip.
- Do more: Housie and festival-chore games, timely festivals, organiser-vs-gang conflict. Do less: costume-only theme parades and Tina show-off reels (two in a row, both flat).
- Our older app-promo Housie posts still pull 5k-110k views; the gang reels have not yet matched them, so the hook is the main problem to solve.

## 2026-10-22 · team feedback on the first reels
- Scripts must flow like a real conversation: reactions, overlapping replies, claim-before-reveal, room tone. A list of call-and-punchline beats feels choppy. Reel length is not a constraint.
- Ideas must be real kitty party scenes (theme days, Housie claims, same saree, gossip after someone leaves, calls from home). Gags invented just for a laugh get rejected.
- One simple situation that anyone gets in 2 seconds. A story that has to be worked out, or follows too many characters, loses people.
- Every frame is a fully dressed set (living room, front door, real phone and camera screens) with props in hands. Bare colour cards look cheap.
- Costumes and theme decor sell the joke visually (Bollywood theme: Chandni, Geet, Basanti, Gabbar).
- Voices: the Hindi voices must say है / हैं as "hae", not a long "haaii" (fixed automatically in build.js). Tina uses a Hindi voice.
- First reel posted: Bollywood theme (KG-2026-10-22-b), 26 s. Its shares and saves set the baseline.
