# Scripts that sound human (the model for every Kitty Gang script)

The team loved these app How-to-play reels because they sound like two real friends: Myra asks naive questions, Tanya answers
casually, every line reacts to the last, small real details (chai lene gayi), nobody sells. Learn the RHYTHM; do not copy the lines
(they are about the app, our reels are not). Since 2026-11-26 every script is judged against these (review.human).

## Ye Kitty Party app hai kya?
- **Myra:** Kya kar rahi hai? Kab se phone mein ghusi hui hai.
- **Tanya:** Housie khel rahi hun! Kitty Party app pe.
- **Myra:** Phone pe Housie? Kaise?
- **Tanya:** Bahut simple hai. Dekh, roz saat games hote hain, jo time sahi lage, Register kar de.
- **Myra:** Bas? Ticket kahan se milega?
- **Tanya:** Woh khud Home pe aa jaata hai. Time hua, Play dabaya, game shuru.
- **Myra:** Arre, ye toh asli wali Housie jaisa hai!
- **Tanya:** Haan na! Number ticket pe ho toh bas tap kar de.
- **Tanya:** Aur dekh... Jhoola poora! Tap kiya, aur points mere.
- **Myra:** Wah! Ye har din aisa hi dikhta hai?
- **Tanya:** Nahi re, roz naya theme. Aaj mela hai, kal kuch aur.
- **Myra:** Mujhe kuch samajh nahi aaya toh?
- **Tanya:** Chat mein Kudrat hai na, usse puch lena.
- **Myra:** Chal, aaj ka game main bhi khelungi!

## Game kaise khelte hain?
- **Myra:** Tanya! Jaldi aa, mera game shuru ho gaya!
- **Tanya:** Arre, itna ghabra kyun rahi hai? Dikha.
- **Myra:** Ye ticket pe Jhoola, Oont... ye sab kya hai?
- **Tanya:** Ye dividends hain. Har dividend ke apne numbers.
- **Tanya:** Ab suno, har das second mein ek number call hota hai.
- **Myra:** 23! Mere paas hai!
- **Tanya:** Toh tap kar na! Haan, ho gaya mark.
- **Myra:** Main chai lene gayi aur number nikal gaya toh?
- **Tanya:** Upar last wale numbers dikhte rehte hain, baad mein mark kar lena.
- **Myra:** Arre, Jhoola ke saare numbers ho gaye!
- **Tanya:** Toh neeche Jhoola daba, jaldi! Koi aur le jayegi!
- **Myra:** Le liya! Main jeet gayi!
- **Tanya:** Aur abhi toh teen Full House baaki hain.

## Ye game chalta kaise hai?
- **Myra:** Ek baat bata, itne saare log ek saath Housie kaise khelte hain?
- **Tanya:** Arre, yahan roz hazaaron log khelte hain.
- **Myra:** Hazaaron? Ek hi game mein?
- **Tanya:** Nahi re! Jitne bhi register karke ticket lete hain, app unke chhote chhote game room bana deta hai.
- **Tanya:** Har room mein 10-20 log. Bilkul ghar wali kitty jaisa.
- **Myra:** Accha! Toh mera bhi ek room hoga?
- **Tanya:** Haan, tera apna room. Usi mein numbers call honge, usi mein jeetna.
- **Myra:** Aur aise kitne room chalte hain?
- **Tanya:** Roz 1000 se bhi zyada!
- **Myra:** Baap re! Toh har game mein itni saari Housie ek saath?
- **Tanya:** Haan, aur kahin bhi bheed nahi. Sabka apna room, apni Housie.
- **Myra:** Wah, ye toh mast hai. Chal, aaj ke room mein milte hain!

## Jeetne pe kya milta hai?
- **Myra:** Dekh, aaj maine Jhoola jeeta!
- **Tanya:** Wah! Aur points dekhe?
- **Myra:** Points?
- **Tanya:** Upar dekh. Har mark pe, har dividend pe Kitty Points milte hain.
- **Myra:** Arre, itne saare! Inka kya karun?
- **Tanya:** Pehle toh leaderboard dekh. Zyada points, toh naam upar.
- **Myra:** Main leaderboard pe? Sab dekhenge?
- **Tanya:** Haan! Aur points jama karke Ticket Pass bhi le sakti hai.
- **Tanya:** 5,000 points mein har game mein 2 tickets, pure 30 din.
- **Myra:** Aur 3 tickets?
- **Tanya:** 10,000 points ka. Friends wale game mein bhi chalta hai.
- **Myra:** Bas, ab main roz khelungi, top pe aana hai!

## What made them human
- Questions a real person asks ("Main chai lene gayi aur number nikal gaya toh?").
- Answers start with a reaction ("Arre, itna ghabra kyun rahi hai?", "Haan na!", "Nahi re").
- Lines of different lengths; nobody speaks in slogans; no laugh sound after every line.
- Real feelings: excitement ("Le liya! Main jeet gayi!"), worry, surprise.
