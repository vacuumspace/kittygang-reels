# Kitty Gang playbook

Final cast and rules for the Kitty Gang Instagram reels. The design canvas page "Kitty Gang" holds the visual version:
https://claude.ai/artifact/TyH5jMemjRZmmnWQa9BD12 (board "Final cast · Kitty Gang" plus Playbook 1 to 5).

## 1. Rulebook: the design system (locked)

- **Shape and finish:** flat, smooth, rounded shapes. No outlines. One darker shade tone on the right side. Soft plum ground shadow. Keep it simple, no decoration for the sake of branding.
- **Body frame:** chibi, big round head (r58) on a 240 x 400 frame. Same shoulders, arm widths, leg length, shoe size and ground line for everyone. Builds change only body width (0.93 to 1.14); the head never changes. Short necks.
- **Arms and hands:** arms taper 19 to 17 to 15 and meet at the elbow with no step. Small human hands (0.95x), three soft finger lines. Hand shape per pose: relaxed, grip, open wave, tucked on hip. Round palms with two lines read as paws: never.
- **Faces:** big glossy eyes with a liner flick, blush, tiny nose. Women wear equal rounded sunglass lenses; the asymmetric logo shades are Kitty's only.
- **Clothes:** modern Delhi wardrobe (saree, kurta set, tee and jeans, co-ord, blazer suit, jumpsuit, athleisure). No frocks. One or two meaningful details per outfit. Sleeves match arm width, cuffs are thin lines, shoes sit in front of hems.
- **Quality check:** render every character at 2.5x and every wrist and elbow at 6x on every build width, list every flaw, fix in one pass, re-check, then publish. When asked to change characters, never change the locked style.
- **Palette:** brand pink #E85D8A, brand yellow #FFD166, plum ink #3A0B30, ground #FFF4F7, gold #F5C451, blush #F7A8C6. Skin tones #F2C2A0, #E8B48E, #C98E66, #B9805C. Outfits in real-world colours, soft warm tones; pink and yellow only as accents.
- **Copy:** no em dashes in captions or on-screen text.
- **Scenes and backgrounds:** be bold with real scene elements (living room, case-file desk, real camera app, chat app, map); never a plain empty card. Solo cards = flat pastel in her colour + soft floor band + 1 or 2 quiet props (framed art, window, plant, lamp). Banned: torans/bunting, decorative circles or dots, rubber stamps. Functional shapes (clocks, Housie balls, buttons) are fine.
- **Staging:** max 3 characters per shot, the rest implied (hand in frame, cropped shoulder, off-screen voice). Characters face whoever is talking (3/4 head turn); nobody stares at camera except in a reverse shot. Seated scenes: the sofa front hides the legs. Group scenes keep one blocking.
- **Audience:** women around 30 to 55 who run or join kitty parties. Simple Hindi/Hinglish, no trend jargon (POV, fit check) in on-screen words, slightly calmer pace and bigger text than Gen Z reels. Post on Instagram, Facebook Reels, WhatsApp Status and YouTube Shorts.

## 3. Expressions and animation

- **Expressions built:** Happy (closed arc eyes, open laugh), Smile, Smirk, Shocked (O mouth), Side-eye, Deadpan (half-lidded, flat). To build before Ep 1: angry, crying-laugh, sleepy, wink.
- **Lip-sync mouths:** mouths follow the loudness of each recorded voice line frame by frame (closed, small, O, open).
- **Body motion:** always alive (breathing, bob, blink every 3 to 5 s, offset per character); talkers turn to the listener and tilt up to 4 degrees; reactions pop (sweat drops, side-eye, jaw drop, laugh shake); no walking (slide, hop or pop in); small squash and stretch on hops.
- **Camera:** Remotion, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, timed to the voice. Pause before every punchline, then punch-in with a small shake; whip cuts between speakers; gentle push-in while talking; something new every 2 to 3 s; format tricks (shutter flash, camera flip, phone clock jump, day to night sky).
- **Poses built:** wave, hold object (phone, calculator, keys, bottle), phone in both hands, hands on hips, holding chai, talking gesture left/right, clap right/left, arms up, laughing hands on tummy, sitting on a sofa (sofa hides legs), 3/4 head turn. To build: arms crossed, gasp, angry, crying-laugh, sleepy, wink.
- **Rig parts:** back hair, body and clothes, upper arms, forearms, hands, legs and feet, head, eyes, brows, mouth, props.

Each line recorded separately and levelled to the same loudness (scripts/voice-cast.js). Real women's voices recorded on a phone are the biggest upgrade and replace these any time.
- **Sound effects:** made in code (reels/scripts/sfx.js + sfx-comedy.js): whoosh, whip, pop, cricket, shutter, ping, record scratch, sting, boing, slurp, thud, heartbeat, calculator, doorbell, creak, clap, crowd. Whoosh on every cut, pop on every sticker, story sounds on top.
- **Music:** composed in code, mood per format (beat pop for quick cuts, suspense strings/ticks for mysteries, soft lo-fi for phone/chat, a speeding loop for time-lapses). Dips under voices, drops out for silences and before punchlines.
- **Sources and mix:** free libraries (Pixabay, Mixkit, Freesound CC0) or made in code; reuse Housie app sounds. No branded notification tones. Voices loudest, effects below, music lowest; final reel around -14 LUFS.

## 5. Script and reel format

- **Format per story, not one template:** many friends one theme = beat-cut line-up with counter; mystery = case file with exhibits and open verdict; something spreading = chain with a progress bar; repeated attempts = camera-app view with attempt counter; waiting/messages = phone chat + live map with clock jumps; never-ending = locked-shot time-lapse loop; comebacks = living-room sitcom.
- **Every reel must have:** text hook in 0 to 2 s (simple Hindi/Hinglish) plus a striking visual; something new every 2 to 3 s; an ending that loops or asks for a comment/share; a ready post caption. Length 15 to 35 s.
- **Screen:** captions only for spoken lines: plain white pill, Fredoka regular (not bold), short, no speaker dot or name. Text hooks as bold pill banners. Nothing in the bottom 20 percent or right edge.
- **Writing:** Hinglish in Roman script, Hindi-first, lines of 8 to 10 words max; every excuse or claim gets a comeback; pause before the punchline; end on a callback or twist; real kitty situations; kind humour (no jokes on bodies, skin colour, caste, religion or money troubles); no em dashes.
- **Story bank (storyboards on the canvas page "AI Reel Ideas"):** Next kitty kiske ghar? (sitcom, Myra hosts with a potluck) · Dress code: PINK (beat-cut line-up, Tanya in yellow) · Housie fixed? (case file) · Gossip telephone (chain, Mrs Sharma twist) · Ek group photo (camera app, front camera twist) · 5 minute mein (chat + map) · The goodbye loop (locked time-lapse) · Bill split (sitcom).
- **No selling inside the story:** reels are about kitty party moments, not app features. No app screens, no download lines, no app mention on the end card.

## 6. Technical notes (for building reels)

- **Project:** Remotion (free tier, individual) in kittyparty/reels. npm run studio = live preview; npx remotion render <Comp> out/x.mp4 (about 2 min).
- **Characters:** generators in reels/characters-src (gen8 + hands + turnpatch for 3/4 head turns + garba outfits/poses); npm run bake makes browser-safe src/kg. Character data objects carry pose, turn, mouth, eyes, outfit colours.
- **Voices:** reels/scripts/voice-cast.js (multi-character, per-line, loudness-levelled, per-frame lip-sync envelope into timing.json).
- **Music and SFX:** reels/scripts/music-quirky.js (and music-garba.js), sfx.js, sfx-comedy.js into public/.
- **Storyboards:** canvas boards built by OneDrive generators (kgreels.js + kgkit.js + stageKit.js). Check frames at zoom before publishing.
- **Output:** 1080 x 1920 MP4 into OneDrive AI Generated/reels. Pipeline notes and lessons: reels/REEL PIPELINE.md.

## 7. Writing room and production pipeline (added 2026-10-21)

- **Pipeline, 3 approval gates:** 1 premise (one line) · 2 script = GATE 1 · 3 storyboard = GATE 2 · 4 voices (levelled, listened through) · 5 animatic (key frames timed to voices with music and SFX) = GATE 3 · 6 full Remotion animation · 7 QA · 8 delivery (MP4 + cover frame + post caption + hashtags in OneDrive, plus what to measure).
- **Beat sheet:** hook (0 to 2 s, a known situation in 6 words or less) · setup (one line) · escalation x3 (rule of three, each with a comeback) · turn (twist flips who wins) · button (callback or deadpan last word) · share trigger lives in the caption, not the story.
- **Punch-up:** write 3 punchlines and keep the best; cut lines that are not setup, joke or turn; be specific (times, rupees, flat numbers, real snacks); each character says what only she would; max one catchphrase per reel; mark pauses as [beat]; read aloud against a timer, over 35 s cut lines (never speed voices); kind-humour check.
- **Script document columns:** Time · Visual · Line (Hinglish caption) · Say (Devanagari for the voice engine) · SFX/music · Beat.
- **Video build:** reusable Remotion scene kits (living room, chat app, camera app, map, case file, line-up, doorway); new props drawn when a story needs them and added to Playbook 6 (96 props + 6 sets now); animatic before animation; half-size preview renders; voices levelled, music auto-ducks, final around -14 LUFS; lip sync from loudness.
- **QA before showing:** full-size frames at hook, every cut, twist, last frame; every voice audible and synced to caption; safe zones; banned list (torans, decorative circles, stamps, speaker dots, em dashes, app selling); characters face the speaker, max 3 per shot.
- **Learn:** log views, watch time, saves, shares, comments, WhatsApp forwards per reel; after 4 to 5 reels keep winning formats/characters and update this playbook; test real women's voices on at least one reel.

## 8. Script quality bar (2026-10-22, after the first autopilot reel was too loose)

- **Shape, 18-25 s, ~50 words:** 0-2 s hook in mid-action (spoken line + visual joke) · 2-6 s setup (one main character with a want) · 6-17 s three escalating beats, each a line + comeback · 17-21 s twist that flips who wins · 21-24 s button that mirrors the opening, flipped, then freeze.
- **Lines of 7 words or less, max 3 talking characters, a laugh or surprise every 5 s, one shot per line or comeback pair, no shot over 4 s.**
- **Scorecard (1-5 each):** hook, relatable, conflict, escalation, laughs, twist, ending, tight, character, shareable. Under 40/50 is rewritten, never rendered.
- **Pace and sound:** voices about 20% faster than their base setting, 0.12 s between lines, music clearly audible (about -15 LUFS between lines, ducked under speech).
- **Automatic QA before posting:** length 15-32 s, pace >= 2.3 words/s, no shot over 4.5 s, scorecard >= 40, loudness -18 to -10 LUFS, music audible. Fails are marked needs-fix and never posted.
